# Failures and recovery

Only failures that actually happened are listed, in the order they happened. Each one says what was tried and how it ended. They happened while the pipeline was being built with a coding agent. Times are East Africa Time.

| Date | Stage / blocker | Attempt | Result | Fix or fallback | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 Nov, 15:20 | Environment. The scaffold's own commands did not run on this Windows machine. | `make validate`, then `python pipeline/validate_storyboard.py`. | `make` is not installed, `python3` is the empty Windows Store stub, and FFmpeg is missing. The validator stopped at `No module named 'jsonschema'`. | A virtual environment inside the repository. One command, `python -m pipeline`. FFmpeg comes from the `imageio-ffmpeg` package, so nothing has to be installed by hand. | `README.md`, "Run it". `requirements.txt`. |
| 25 Nov, 15:55 | Assembly, radio cut, in a dry run. The sound mix did not stop. | Mix the narration over a placeholder score. | It wrote a 9.5 GB file until the drive was full: `No space left on device`. | Reproduced with the output sent nowhere, in seven variants. The score was set to repeat for ever, and the ducking filter waits for every stream to end. The score is now repeated only as often as the film needs, and the mix has a hard stop at the film's length. The mix ends at 63.00 s. | `pipeline/assemble.py`, `mix_audio`. `ARCHITECTURE.md`, "What can fail". |
| 25 Nov, 16:00 | Assembly, film. The join could not find its shots. | Join the shot files with FFmpeg's concat list. | `Impossible to open '4e40…mp4'`, and later `Error opening input file …picture.txt`. | 25 Nov, 16:02 | Assembly, film. The end card never appeared. | Lay the end card over the joined picture. | The picture ended at 60 s while the sound ran to 63 s: 1,440 frames instead of 1,512. An overlay cannot outlast the picture it is laid on. | The last frame is held for the length of the end card. Every film is now counted frame by frame before it is trusted. | `pipeline/assemble.py`, `film` and `_frame_count`. |
| 25 Nov, 16:05 | Assembly, film. A shot made from a video clip crashed. | Bring a 3-second clip to the length of a 7.5-second shot. | `UnidentifiedImageError` on an `.mp4`. The clip's size was read as if it were a still image. | The size is read only for stills that need it. A clip's last frame is held for the rest of its shot. | `pipeline/assemble.py`, `segment`. |
| 25 Nov, 16:06 | Assembly, film. The blurred backdrop failed. | Show a still whole, over a blurred copy of itself. | `Invalid chroma_param radius value 8, must be >= 0 and < 8`. The blur was too strong for the colour detail of the small copy. | A radius of 4 on both. The copy is shrunk first, so the blur stays strong and costs little. | `pipeline/assemble.py`, `_blurred_backdrop`. |
| 25 Nov, 16:06 | Assembly, film. A broken shot was taken for a finished one. | Join eight shots, one of them left half-written by an earlier failed attempt. | FFmpeg kept only the first shot and still exited with code 0. The film had 252 frames instead of 1,512. The cache had asked only whether a file existed. | Every media file is written under a temporary name and moved into place only when it is complete. The joined picture and each film are counted frame by frame. | `pipeline/assemble.py`, `_render`, `_concat` and `_frame_count`. |
| 25 Nov, 17:00 | Measurement. The list-price estimate of an image was too high. | Read the first two lines of `runs.jsonl`. | The smoke image was logged at $0.000346. One input tile too many was counted when there were no reference images. The right figure is $0.000287. | The estimate counts only the reference images that exist, and uses Cloudflare's exact figures. The log line was left as it was written. | `pipeline/providers/cloudflare_flux.py`. `runs.jsonl`, line 2. |
| 25 Nov, 22:07 | Score, on Kaggle. The job stopped while importing the model. | The first run of the 15-second test, on a T4 GPU. | Kaggle's Python is 3.13. The private Python 3.11 environment was built in 72 seconds, with 142 packages. Then `ValueError: Key backend: 'module://matplotlib_inline.backend_inline' is not a valid value for backend`. Kaggle's notebook passes `MPLBACKEND`, its own plotting backend, to the programs it starts, and that backend is not in the private environment. | The job's environment leaves out the notebook's Python settings and uses `Agg`. Confirmed on Kaggle: run again in the same session, the job then made 15 seconds of music in 10.7 seconds on the T4, with 5.6 GB of GPU memory at its peak. | `kaggle/make_score.py`, `clean_environment`. `kaggle/logs/score-smoke-run-1.txt`, and `kaggle/logs/score-smoke-run-2/progress.log`. |
| 25 Nov, 23:04 | Narration. The free speech allowance ran out. | Speak the twelve lines, one request each. | After five calls in a row the service answered HTTP 429, then 503, then 429 again. The retries waited 44 s and 30 s, as the service asked, and both lines then came out. At the twelfth line it refused with `Retry-After: 42188`. Its message: "limit: 10 requests per day on Free Tier". Eleven lines were made. | The service states its limit as 10 requests a day, and its wait ends at 08:00 UTC (11:00 East Africa Time). The last line is to be made after that. Speech is now budgeted: one request per line, and no try-outs of other narrators. | `runs.jsonl`, the narration lines of 25 Nov. The two `probe` lines that read the message. |
| 25 Nov, 23:15 | Narration. A run sat still for over ten minutes and showed nothing. | Find out what the narration run was doing. | It had not hung on the network: the service answered a fresh request at once. A sample of the running program's stack (`py-spy dump`) showed it asleep in the retry loop. It was obeying a server-asked wait of nearly twelve hours, and the notice of it was held back because the run's output was piped through another program. | A server-asked wait is obeyed up to two minutes. A longer one stops the run and names the time to return. The wait is remembered in `outputs/_build/`, so no more calls are sent to be refused before then. Long runs are no longer piped through another program. | `pipeline/retry.py`, `with_retries`. `outputs/_build/wait-speech.json`. |
| 25 Nov, 23:16 | Logging. Three hand-sent requests nearly went unlogged. | Read the service's limit message with a request sent by hand. | The first was answered with speech. The second was refused, and the hand-written script crashed before it wrote its line. A rule of this project is that every call is logged. | The probe was rewritten as a file, and the third request was logged as it returned. The first two were written to `runs.jsonl` afterwards, each saying so. All three are under the stage `probe`. | `runs.jsonl`, stage `probe`. |
| 25 Nov, 23:19 | Measurement. The voice-pitch check read three lines an octave high. | Measure the narrator's pitch with `python -m pipeline check`. | The calm lines read 98 to 143 Hz. The excited lines read 168 to 216 Hz. The rule that picks the period was taking the one above it on bright vowels. | A stricter rule moved the figures by a few hertz and no more. The check reports what it finds and says nothing about whether the voice is a man's or a woman's. That is left to the ear. | `pipeline/audio_check.py`, `_pitch`. |
| 25 Nov, 23:21 | Timing. The spoken lines were longer than the film. | Cut the film with the first planned lengths. | Two lines ran past their shots (8.29 s and 8.01 s of speech). With the end card the film would have run 92.8 s. The limit is 90 s. The storyboard's own check had passed, because it checks planned times, and those are only a floor. | The real length is checked after the lines are spoken, and the pipeline refuses to assemble a film outside 60 to 90 s. The planned lengths were re-balanced to 85.5 s. The film now runs 89.4 s. | `pipeline/assemble.py`, `_prepare`. `EXPERIMENTS.md`, 3. |
| 25 Nov, 23:27 | Pictures. The milk container stood up again after it had fallen. | Look at all twelve keyframes together, on one sheet. | In shots 09, 10 and 12 the container was upright, though the boy had knocked it over in shot 06. | The three prompts now name the container on its side and the spilt milk. | 25 Nov, 23:40 | Repository. A privacy rule hid the wrong folder. | Keep the photographs of the two people out of the public repository, with a rule in `.gitignore`. | The rule was written `references/`. Git applies a rule of that form to any folder with that name, so it also hid `outputs/references/`: the small copies of the photographs that the pipeline needs, and the generated picture of the place. A clean clone would have had neither, and could not have run, while three documents said it could. Nothing had been committed, so nothing was lost. | The rule is anchored to the root: `/references/`. Both cases are now checked with `git check-ignore` before any commit, and a clean copy of the repository was tried. | `.gitignore`. `ETHICS.md`, "The photographs are not published". |
| 25 Nov, 23:45 | Dry run, in a clean copy of the repository. The dry run stopped on a photograph. | Run `python -m pipeline film --mock` in a copy of exactly what a fresh clone would hold: with the small copies of the photographs, and without the photographs. | The keyframes and their copies were all found and reused, and nothing was called. Tried again in the clean copy: both films written, 2,052 frames each, and nothing called. | `pipeline/keyframes.py`, `from_photo` and `run`. |
| 26 Nov, about 00:45 | Pictures.
